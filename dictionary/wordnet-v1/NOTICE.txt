ReadFlow general dictionary v1

Adapted from Princeton WordNet 3.0 and Chinese Open Wordnet (COW, OMW 1.4).
ReadFlow combines English WordNet glosses and Chinese Open Wordnet lexical
equivalents by the shared synset identifier, normalizes headwords for lookup,
and distributes compact JSON shards with up to 8 senses per headword.
An English gloss may have no Chinese equivalent in the source. The Chinese
equivalents are lexical matches, and may need context to select the correct one.
