ReadFlow selects word, phonetic, part-of-speech statistics, Chinese and English
definition text, and inflections from the source CSV. Lookup keys are normalized,
duplicate lookup keys retain the first usable row, and backslash-escaped line
breaks are decoded. Inflection aliases are generated from ECDICT's exchange column.
Chinese and English definitions keep their independent source ordering.

The ECDICT maintainer states the dictionary may be bundled in an offline app
under MIT. The project combines data from multiple sources and no longer has
entry-level source records. The upstream permissions have not all been verified.
